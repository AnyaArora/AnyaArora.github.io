The best and worst books of 2026 thus far

The Hate U Give by Angie Thomas
Great Social Commentary

This is an excellent book that dives into incredibly important topics without holding the reader's hand. Every aspect is great, from the story to the characters to the writing. Incredibly interesting to analyze, even if I had to read it for school.






Projekt 1065 by Alan Gratz
Too Simple for Me

Perhaps not the best book to recommend for high school students. The plot was very predictable and I found myself hoping that it was almost over. The writing was a little basic and childish. May be a good way to introduce hard topics to younger students.






The Perks of Being a Wallflower by Stephen Chobsky
Incredibly Emotional

This book has been on my TBR for far too long so bless Leah Park for letting me borrow her copy. I have no words. This book is beautiful. In the quiet meaningful sort of way that leaves you thinking and questioning and introspecting. Such a beauty of prose. Just all the reasons I love books like The Catcher In The Rye . A wonderful novel that is just so sadly relatable






Thirteen Reasons Why by Jay Asher
Great Storyline

This book has a very strong plot with great storytelling, but the writing itself was not amazing. The way it balances past and present is done excellently, even if the writing is tad simple. A short read that gets you thinking.

1984 by George Orwell
So Terribly Relevent

A great way to getting thinking about the status of our country. The misogyny is a little strong, which can make it hard to read for some, but it can be seen as a result of the society within the book. The ending is amazing, with great philosphical points about the true meaning of existence, and whether reality is only a fiction of the mind. The themes of surviellence when analyzed in the context of AI can be haunting, as it should be.
